BACKGROUND

Graduate student at the University of Pennsylvania (M.S., expected 2028), and a graduate of Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute with dual degrees in Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering. I work at the boundary of hardware and software, where design decisions at the silicon level ripple through to running systems.

During my internship at Kulicke & Soffa, I gained industry experience in semiconductor back-end assembly and advanced chip packaging. At RPI I contributed to undergraduate research in robotics and computer hardware.
